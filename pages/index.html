Why Sustainable Transportation Matters

Due to wars and political conflicts in oil-producing countries can disrupt oil supplies and cause fuel prices to rise sharply. When oil prices increase, transportation costs also increase, making daily travel, food delivery, and goods more expensive. This can affect both businesses and ordinary people. However, having a sustainable transportation such as public transport, electric vehicles, cycling, and renewable energy, we can reduce the reliance on oil and become more energy secure.

Better Ways to Travel

  1. Public transport Using buses and MRT trains reduces the number of private cars on the road, which helps lower air pollution, save fuel, and reduce traffic congestion.
  2. Cycling and walking Cycling and walking do not require fuel and produce no harmful gas emissions, making them environmentally friendly ways to travel.
  3. Electric vehicles Electric vehicles use electricity instead of petrol or diesel, helping reduce dependence on oil and lowering carbon emissions.

Benefit of Green Transportation

Cleaner transportation methods help improving public health by reduce the number of people getting respiratory diseases caused by polluted air. In addition, green transportation helps reduce traffic congestion because fewer private vehicles are needed on the roads. Public transport systems such as buses and MRT trains can carry many passengers at once, making travel more efficient and reducing traffic jams.
