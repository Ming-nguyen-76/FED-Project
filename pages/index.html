Better Ways to Travel

Public transport

Using buses and MRT trains reduces the number of private cars on the road, which helps lower air pollution, save fuel, and reduce traffic congestion.

Cycling and walking

Cycling and walking do not require fuel and produce no harmful gas emissions, making them environmentally friendly ways to travel.

Electric vehicles

Electric vehicles use electricity instead of petrol or diesel, helping reduce dependence on oil and lowering carbon emissions.

Why Sustainable Transportation Matters

Wars and political conflicts in oil-producing countries can reduce oil supply and cause fuel prices to rise. This makes transport, food delivery, and goods more expensive for everyone. By using sustainable transportation such as public transport, electric vehicles, cycling, and renewable energy, we can depend less on oil and improve energy security.

Benefit of Green Transportation

Cleaner transportation methods help improving public health by reduce the number of people getting respiratory diseases caused by polluted air. In addition, green transportation helps reduce traffic congestion because fewer private vehicles are needed on the roads. Public transport systems such as buses and MRT trains can carry many passengers at once, making travel more efficient and reducing traffic jams.
