Basis Information

Coming from Vietnam, country that faced serious problem with sustainable transportation. As a year 1 polytechnic student that interested in green transportation, I have encouraged my family and friends to change from using fuel cars to electric cars. This have significantly helped my family to pass through the oil crisis as we do not have to worry too much about the price of fuel. I feel that this is a very good practice and very easy to change.
