Personal Action

Personal Experience

As a student from Viet Nam, I grew up seeing busy roads filled with motorbikes, cars, and buses every morning. Therefore, sustainable transportation is quite a serious problem in our country One day, my school encouraged students to use greener ways of travelling, so I decided to take the bus more often and walk when the distance was short. At first, it felt inconvenient, but I felt proud because I was helping to reduce pollution and save energy.

Goals

01

Encourage more people to use public transport for daily travelling.

02

Promote walking and cycling as cleaner ways to travel short distances.

03

Support the use of electric vehicles to reduce fuel consumption and pollution.

04

Reduce dependence on oil and move towards cleaner, renewable energy sources.

Challenges

  • Lack of community awareness
  • Building public transport systems is expensive and needs a lot of investment.
  • When cities get more crowded, transport systems may not be able to handle so many people.
