Personal Action

Personal Experience

As a student from Viet Nam, I grew up seeing busy roads filled with motorbikes, cars, and buses every morning. Therefore, sustainable transportation is quite a serious problem in our country One day, my school encouraged students to use greener ways of travelling, so I decided to take the bus more often and walk when the distance was short. At first, it felt inconvenient, but I felt proud because I was helping to reduce pollution and save energy.

Goal

My goal is to help people to change their habit of travelling. Not only for Singaporean, but also people from my country and people in the world.

Challenges

  • People may not fully understand the importance of sustainable transportation or how their travel choices affect the environment.
  • Building public transport systems requires a large amount of money and investment.
  • Cities become more crowded, transportation systems may struggle to handle the increasing number of commuters.
